# Demonstration findings
These results come from fictional seed-42 data and describe no real business.

- 180 projects: 160 completed and 20 active.
- 93 completed projects finished late: 58.1% of completed projects.
- 77 completed projects exceeded their recorded budgets.

An analyst would inspect the most delayed jobs and the largest budget variances, then investigate scope changes, project complexity and actual labor hours. This dataset lacks those variables, so it cannot explain why delays or overruns occurred. Crew completion ranks describe assignment throughput, not efficiency. Pending permit records are excluded from approval-time averages and reported separately.

Reproduce with `python app.py --report results.json`. `sample_results.json` provides all six query outputs for review before running the application.
